Privacy Policy

Noir Run: Wingsuit Escape. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Noir Run collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no server. Nothing about you, or about how you play, ever leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your best distance and your settings. How far you've got, which levels that has opened, and the difficulty and sliders you chose are saved on the device so the game opens where you left it.
  • Device motion. You steer, speed up and slow down by tilting the phone. The game reads which way is down from the motion sensors, frame by frame, and uses it only to move you. It isn't recorded or sent anywhere. Reading it needs no permission.

Permissions

None. Noir Run doesn't ask for the camera, the microphone, your photos, your location or your contacts, and doesn't use them.

Third parties

None. The game contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no ads and no crash reporters, and nothing in it talks to the network.

Children

Since the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your best distance and settings with it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
